## Decision summary

The owner has decided to build an MVP aimed at the most common problems of home growers (D01), covering vegetables, berry bushes and fruit trees with pruning tasks (D02), within 25 hours of build time (D06), for the owner's own garden and the family gardens they know first-hand (their mother's and grandmother's), without interviewing other gardeners (D11, which supersedes D05).
The only real problem account is the owner's own: with many plants it is easy to lose track of what to do and when, namely when to sow, plant out, prune and which spray to apply; companion planting is a problem and pruning needs care. No episode, frequency or cost has been described, and the owner calls the problem somewhat "hypothetical" while treating it as real (D11). See Problems and Target group.
Now, decided by the owner on 2026-10-20 in this order, which is also the cut order if 25 hours run out (D07): 1. a calendar and task list (sow, plant out, prune) from frost dates and the sourced catalogue; 2. square-foot beds with companion warnings; 3. one AI feature, answering questions about the user's own garden from catalogue data (D08). The task list shows what needs doing (D12): derived tasks from Now item 1 (D07, R05), plus operations the gardener adds, planned or already done, kept in a separate list where one operation can cover many plants (D14), and success is the owner's own test (D13). Spraying advice, the journal with photos, rotation history, photo diagnosis and the AI layout proposal are Later (D08, D09). All three choices began as agent proposals; the order follows the owner's own task list, not research. See Scope.
What could change this: the problem is the owner's account alone, across three family gardens; whether other growers share it is accepted untested (A01, D11), so the result speaks for this family, not for home growers. The decided Now may not fit 25 hours, and the AI answer is the first thing cut (A02), which would leave how to prune uncovered, since item 1 gives only when (accepted by the owner, D07); a wrong AI pruning answer could harm a plant (A05); pruning and companion data are owner-written (A03, D04); trees may not fit the grid (A04). Binding `AGENTS.md` still says "vegetable-garden planner" with journal, photos and AI layout suggestions in MVP, so D02, D08, D09 and D10, and R09's stored operations (no such record or owner in `AGENTS.md`, which persists only overrides), conflict with project rules until `AGENTS.md` and the engineering standards are updated.
Next: `om-backlog .ai/specs/product-brief.md --dry-run` to draft epics and stories for Now (D07): the task list per D12 and D14 first, the grid phases gated on A04 (#13), the AI answer last. Issue #18 (interviews) is obsolete under D11. The owner's own test of the MVP (D13) feeds the next refresh. Dates are not set.

## Vision

For home growers with vegetables, berry bushes and fruit trees, Plant.AI is meant to keep track of what to do in their own garden and when, so a growing garden doesn't get lost; this is the team's intended direction, not a demonstrated benefit. `[DOCUMENT]` D01, D02

- As a garden grows, it becomes easy to lose track of what to do, even for an experienced grower. One account, no episode or cost given. `[INTERVIEW]` owner, 2026-10-19
- Knowing when to do certain tasks is a problem. `[INTERVIEW]` owner, 2026-10-19
- What the owner keeps track of: when to sow, then when to plant out, when to prune, and which spray to apply. A list, not an episode. `[INTERVIEW]` owner, 2026-10-20 addendum
- Companion planting ("good neighbours") is a problem; what exactly goes wrong was not described. `[INTERVIEW]` owner, 2026-10-19
- Pruning needs care because some plants (the owner named *Prunus*) may not grow back after a cut. `[INTERVIEW]` owner, 2026-10-19
- Generic frost advice, contradictory companion lists, lost previous-season notes, unidentified plant disease and rotation mistakes appear only in illustrative material. `[ASSUMPTION]` survey and interviews, 2026-10-14 to 2026-10-18
- Person, situation, outcome: a grower whose number of plants keeps growing wants to keep track of what to do and when. All four items the owner listed on 2026-10-20 concern timing or treatment, none placement; that is the owner's account, not a pattern across growers (A01). A "this week" list is a proposed framing from illustrative material, not the owner's words; see Q07.

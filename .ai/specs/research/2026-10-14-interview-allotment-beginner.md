# Interview — first-season allotment holder (ROD), 2026-10-14
- Situation: took over an allotment in March; planted tomatoes, cucumbers and courgettes "where there was space"; tomatoes went in on 5 May and two plants froze in the 12 May cold snap.
- What they did: a YouTube channel, a seed-packet back, a Facebook group for allotment holders, and ChatGPT ("it told me April was fine, it was not").
- Cost: replanted tomatoes from the market, "about 80 zł and one weekend"; courgettes shaded half the bed.
- Tried before: a paper notebook with a hand-drawn plan, lost by June.
- Problem gone when: "someone tells me on Monday what to do this week, for my plot, not for California."
- Quotes: "Every packet says 'after the last frost'. Which one?" "I don't need a pretty drawing, I need a list."
- Did not care about: exact planting plans to the centimetre; sharing the garden with others.

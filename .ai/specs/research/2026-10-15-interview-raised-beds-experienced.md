- What they did: leafs through several years of notebooks; neighbour relations from memory, the allotment club and own notes; tried one English app, deleted it because its calendar was wrong for her valley.
- Cost: one whole bed lost to clubroot after a rotation mistake; hours of leafing every February.
- Tried before: an English garden app (calendar "all in March"), paper.
- Problem gone when: in February the app shows where each crop was in the last three years and warns "not cabbage here".
- Quotes: "proponować może. sadzić będę ja"; "kapustnych nie dajesz w to samo miejsce przez trzy, cztery lata"; "w internecie każdy pisze co innego"; "po zimnej Zośce… czasem dopiero koniec maja".
- Did not care about: AI layouts as anything more than a suggestion; frequent notifications; frequent logging in.
- Interviewer's remarks: rotation history came up unprompted and three times; it is not in the current scope. Distrusts generic companion-planting data, trusts her own records. Local frost date differs from the regional rule by about two weeks. Photos wanted only if they attach themselves to a bed.

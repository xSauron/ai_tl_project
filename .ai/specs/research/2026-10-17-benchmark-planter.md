# Benchmark — Planter: Garden Planner, checked 2026-10-17 by the project owner

- Link: https://planter.garden and the App Store listing https://apps.apple.com/app/id1542642210
- What it does for our users' job, in one paragraph: the closest product to Plant.AI and the reason the idea exists. Arranges a garden on a square-foot grid, shows companion and combative plants, and has a planting calendar for starting seeds and transplanting. The website describes "80+ fruits and vegetables and hundreds of varieties"; the App Store listing says 100+ and thousands of varieties, and custom plants can be added. Available on iOS, Android and web.
- What it does well: square-foot spacing makes a plan finishable by a beginner; companion information sits next to the plan instead of in a separate article; one app across phone and web.
- Where it falls short for our users: listing language is English only; nothing on the pages checked says how frost dates are set or whether the calendar fits a Polish frost valley; no AI layout proposal and no photo diagnosis described; rotation history not described (not checked in the app itself).
- Pricing or model, as shown on the date checked: free download with in-app purchases; the App Store lists monthly ($4.99), annual ($19.99, $24.99, legacy $11.99), "Planter Premium" ($11.99) and lifetime ($39.99, $49.99) options; the listing does not say which features each unlocks.
- Ratings: 4.7 out of 5 from about 2.2K ratings on the App Store.
- Screens worth keeping as references: the bed grid with plant icons per square, the companion / combative hint while placing a plant. Functional and UX benchmark only: do not copy its branding, texts, plant data or images.
- Not checked: Android listing, the web app behind login, actual frost-date handling, which features are paid.

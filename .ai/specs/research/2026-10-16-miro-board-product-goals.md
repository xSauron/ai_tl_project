# Discovery board (Miro) — Product, Project Goals. Exported 2026-10-16 by the project owner

Document type: the owner's own discovery board; positions, not evidence.

## Product (what it is, what stands out, feasibility)
- Product: web app, mobile-first, to plan vegetable beds on a square-foot grid.
- Product: crop catalogue with spacing, timing and companion / combative relations.
- Product: sowing and transplant calendar computed from the user's frost dates.
- Product: garden journal with notes and photos per bed and planting.
- Product: AI layout proposal for a bed from a list of crops the user wants.
- Stands out: calendar for the user's own frost dates, not a US default.
- Stands out: Polish language and Polish growing conventions (allotments, "zimni ogrodnicy").
- Stands out: AI never writes to the plan without the user pressing apply.
- Feasible: grid, catalogue and calendar are plain CRUD plus pure functions.
- Feasible: AI proposal is one prompt plus validation against the grid rules; a mock in tests.
- Feasible: MVP in 25 hours if photo diagnosis and rotation history are deferred.

## Project Goals (it is a hobby project: what "done" means for the owner)
- Goal: finish the course with a working app built through the om-* pipeline end to end.
- Goal: plan the owner's own beds in it for the 2027 season.
- Goal: at least three real gardeners open it more than once during spring 2027.
- Goal: practise one AI feature properly (versioned prompt, validated output, mock in CI).
- Non-goal: revenue, ads, a seed shop, growth targets.

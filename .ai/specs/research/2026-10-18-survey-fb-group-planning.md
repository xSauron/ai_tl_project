# Survey — "how did you plan this season", posted in a Facebook group for allotment and home vegetable growers 2026-10-15, closed 2026-10-18, run by the project owner

Illustrative course material (see the manifest): the numbers show the shape a real survey would take; they were not collected from real people.

Where: one Polish Facebook group for allotment and home growers (about 12,000 members), a post with a form link, three days. Anonymous form. 47 responses. Nobody was told what Plant.AI is; the post said "krótka ankieta o planowaniu warzywnika".

Questions and answers, counts as exported:

1. Where do you grow vegetables? (one)
   - allotment (ROD): 19 · garden at a house: 17 · balcony or terrace only: 9 · other: 2

2. How did you plan this season's beds? (multiple choice)
   - in my head: 28 · on paper: 21 · photo of last year: 9 · spreadsheet: 3 · an app: 4 · I didn't plan: 8

3. What went wrong this season that a plan could have prevented? (multiple choice)
   - planted out too early / frost: 18 · plants too close or shaded: 22 · bad neighbours: 7 · same crop in the same place (rotation): 11 · disease I couldn't identify: 24 · nothing: 6

4. When do you plant out tomatoes? (one)
   - before 1 May: 3 · 1–14 May: 9 · after 15 May ("zimni ogrodnicy"): 29 · it depends on the year: 6

5. Would you use an app that suggests a bed layout? (one)
   - yes, and I'd follow it: 5 · yes, as a starting point I change: 23 · no, I decide myself: 14 · don't know: 5

6. What would make you open such an app again in spring? (multiple choice)
   - a list of what to do this week: 31 · last year's plan and notes: 26 · help with sick plants from a photo: 29 · layout suggestions: 12 · reminders: 15

7. Would you pay for it?
   - no: 30 · a one-off small amount: 13 · a subscription: 4

Reading aid, project owner, 2026-10-18: 41 of 47 named at least one problem a plan or a diagnosis could prevent; "disease I couldn't identify" (24) and "too close or shaded" (22) led, "bad neighbours" was last (7). Layout suggestions were wanted mostly as a starting point (23), rarely as something to follow (5). Only 4 plan in an app today. Paying was mostly no (30), which fits the hobby goals.

Limits: one group, self-selected, three days, stated intentions; the question order may have primed question 6.

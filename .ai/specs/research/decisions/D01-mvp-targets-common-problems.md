# D01 — The MVP aims to solve the most common problems home growers have
- Date, owner: 2026-10-19, project owner (stated in the om-discover session)
- Context and the options weighed: the session was asked to choose between locking a provisional MVP scope and first checking beliefs with real gardeners; the owner answered "We are trying to do MVP so solve most common problems."
- Decision and why: build an MVP aimed at the most common problems. Which problems are most common is not yet established by real material (the survey and three interviews are illustrative; see `../2026-10-18-source-manifest.md`); the owner's own account (`../interviews/2026-10-19-owner-trained-gardener.md`) names losing track of tasks, timing, companion planting and careful pruning.
- Consequences, and what would make us revisit it: "most common" stays a hypothesis until real growers other than the owner are heard; revisit if they report different problems.
- Status: active

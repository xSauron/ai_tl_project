# D13 — MVP success is checked by the owner's own hands-on test
- Date, owner: 2026-10-20, project owner. Written chat, Polish: "Poznam jak sam przetestuje system i ocenie czego brakuje a co dziala dobrze" (I'll know when I test the system myself and judge what is missing and what works well). Answer to Q03.
- Context and the options weighed: the earlier proposal ("three real gardeners open it more than once in spring 2027") no longer fit D11. The agent offered three replacements: the owner's 2027 beds and spring task list, the family gardens too, a finished course. The owner chose none of them as worded and gave their own check.
- Decision and why: the MVP is checked by the owner using it on their own garden and writing down what is missing and what works well. No numeric threshold and no stop rule were set.
- Consequences, and what would make us revisit it:
  - The check is qualitative and made by the person who builds the product, so it can't show that anyone else needs it (A01, D11).
  - The owner's notes from the test become an `[INTERVIEW]` account for the next `om-discover --refresh`.
  - Revisit if the app goes beyond the family.
- Status: active

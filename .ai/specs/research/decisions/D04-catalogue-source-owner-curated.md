# D04 — The catalogue is owner-curated and cited, backed by Wikidata and Trefle
- Date, owner: 2026-10-19, project owner (accepted the agent's proposal in full: "Przyjmuję w całości"; pruning check: "Tylko ja")
- Context and the options weighed: `research/2026-10-19-data-request-catalogue-source.md` assessed 9 candidate sources. No candidate with a clean licence covers companion relations or pruning windows; OpenFarm, Growstuff, Permapeople and PFAF were rejected (no reuse licence, or another app's data) and Planter is excluded by `AGENTS.md`. The agent proposed an owner-curated catalogue with open datasets as backing, and offered a second horticulture checker for pruning or the owner checking alone as an accepted risk.
- Decision and why: answers Q01.
  1. Identity and taxonomy come from Wikidata (CC0); the Wikidata QID is the stable species reference.
  2. Spacing and days-to-harvest come from Trefle (CC-BY-4.0) only where a record exists and its upstream licence allows reuse; `source` names the record and the app credits "Data: Trefle.io (CC-BY-4.0)".
  3. Frost offsets, companion relations and pruning windows are written by the owner from published references (books, official methodologies, extension-service guides), with a bibliographic citation in `source` for every fact. Entries are compiled from several references and never bulk-copied from one database (EU database right, Directive 96/9/EC).
  4. The owner, a trained gardener, authors every entry and checks pruning entries alone. No second checker is named. This is an **accepted risk**: a wrong pruning window can kill some plants (*Prunus* in the owner's account), and nobody else reviews it before it ships.
- Consequences, and what would make us revisit it: supersedes D03; catalogue seed work (`packages/db/src/seeds/`) is unblocked. Companion and pruning data are authoring work for the owner, not an import, and must be estimated against the 25-hour budget (A02, Q04); if they do not fit, pruning windows leave Now under D02. The PIORiN/IO-PIB methodologies stay an optional cited reference until their availability and licence status are confirmed. Revisit if a cleanly licensed companion or pruning dataset appears, if Trefle's terms change, or if a pruning entry turns out wrong (then add a second checker).

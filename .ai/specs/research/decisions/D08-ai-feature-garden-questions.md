# D08 — The one AI feature in MVP answers questions about the user's own garden; the AI layout proposal moves to Later
- Date, owner: 2026-10-20, project owner (accepted the agent's recommendation with "okej" and confirmed it as worded at sign-off: "Zgadzam sie ze wszystim")
- Context and the options weighed: `AGENTS.md` allows AI for layout suggestions and for garden Q&A. (a) AI layout proposal: its output can be checked against grid rules, but none of the owner's problems is about layout. (b) Answers to questions about the user's own garden, such as when or how to prune a given plant: closer to the owner's timing and pruning problems; the companion (placement) problem is served by the beds' warnings instead; a wrong pruning answer can harm a plant. The agent leaned towards (b), stating that this was its judgement, not research.
- Decision and why: (b). Answers draw on catalogue facts, are labelled as suggestions and never change the plan (R06).
- Consequences, and what would make us revisit it: `AGENTS.md` already allows garden Q&A; the first implementing PR must update `AGENTS.md` and the engineering standards where they assume the layout proposal ships: the product description, the proposal/apply rules, grid-rule validation of AI output (no equivalent exists for free-text answers), and the integration test for double-applying a proposal. The AI endpoint must be rate-limited like every AI endpoint. Q&A output is harder to validate than a layout checked against grid rules; answer safety is tested under A05. Revisit if A05 fails or after the D05 interviews.

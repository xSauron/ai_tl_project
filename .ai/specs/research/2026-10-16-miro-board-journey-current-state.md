# Discovery board (Miro) — Current-state journey: grower plans a season. Exported 2026-10-16 by the project owner

Document type: the owner's journey map. Persona: a home vegetable grower. Scenario: planning the beds for next season. Trigger: seed catalogues arrive in January. Goal: a plan for what goes where and when, and a season that goes better than the last one. Scope: from planning in winter to looking back after harvest. The stages restate the three interviews; thoughts and emotions are the owner's reconstruction.

| Stage | Goal | Actions | Touchpoints | Thoughts | Emotions | Problems |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Looking back | remember last season | leafs through notebooks and photos | paper notebook, phone gallery | "Where were the cabbages?" | nostalgia, doubt | history is scattered across years and media |
| Choosing crops | decide what to grow | browses seed catalogues and packets | catalogues, garden centre, FB groups | "Will this grow here?" | excitement | packets assume another climate |
| Laying out beds | decide what goes where | draws beds on paper | notebook, by eye | "Can these go together?" | uncertainty | companion advice contradicts itself |
| Timing | know when to sow and plant out | reads packets, asks the club, asks ChatGPT | packets, allotment club, AI chat | "Which last frost?" | anxiety | generic dates, wrong for a frost valley |
| Planting | put plants in | sows and transplants | the garden | "Too early?" | hope | cold snap in mid-May kills early plants |
| Tending | keep plants healthy | waters, watches leaves, takes photos | phone camera, FB groups | "Why are the leaves yellow?" | worry | no clear diagnosis, fear of being sold a spray |
| Harvest and review | learn for next year | notes what worked | notebook (sometimes) | "I'll remember this." | satisfaction or disappointment | notes are not taken or get lost |

# Discovery board (Miro) — Vision, Target Group, Needs. Exported 2026-10-16 by the project owner

Document type: the owner's own discovery board. Sticky notes are the owner's positions, not user statements; where a note restates an interview, the interview note is the source.

## Vision
- Motivation: every season home growers repeat the same mistakes because what they learned last year lives in a notebook, a camera roll and their head.
- Change: one place that holds the plan, the history and the calendar for your own garden, with an AI that suggests and never decides.
- One sentence: plan the beds you actually have, for the frost dates you actually get, and remember what worked.

## Target Group (which market, who are customers and users)
- Market: free and freemium garden-planning apps for home vegetable growers.
- Competitors named: Planter, GrowVeg / Almanac Garden Planner, paper notebooks.
- User: allotment (ROD) holder in their first or second season.
- User: experienced grower with raised beds who plans in winter.
- User: balcony grower with boxes and pots.
- Beachhead: Polish-speaking vegetable growers on allotments and raised beds.

## Needs (which problem, what benefit)
- Problem: generic advice ("after the last frost") does not say when, for my place.
- Problem: companion-planting advice online contradicts itself.
- Problem: last year's plan and results are lost or hard to find.
- Problem: AI chat answers sound confident and are often wrong for the local climate.
- Benefit: a this-week list for my beds, from my frost dates.
- Benefit: a bed plan that warns about bad neighbours before I plant.
- Benefit: a journal that keeps what was where and what happened.
- Benefit: an AI layout proposal I can preview, edit, accept or throw away.

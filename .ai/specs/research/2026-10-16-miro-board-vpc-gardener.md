# Discovery board (Miro) — Value Proposition Canvas, Home grower. Exported 2026-10-16 by the project owner

Document type: the owner's canvas. Jobs, pains and gains are hypotheses about home growers; the three interviews (2026-10-14, 10-15, 10-16) are the only user statements behind them, and they are illustrative course material.

Customer segment: home vegetable grower on an allotment, raised beds or a balcony.
Value proposition: a plan for the beds you have, timed to your frost dates, with last year remembered.

## Jobs
- Decide what goes where this season.
- Know when to sow, start indoors and plant out.
- Avoid planting crops next to bad neighbours.
- Remember what was where and how it went.
- Find out why a plant looks sick.

## Pains
- Seed packets and articles assume a climate that is not mine.
- Companion-planting lists contradict each other.
- Last year's plan is in a lost notebook or scattered photos.
- AI chat answers sound sure and are wrong for my place.
- Apps are in English, in feet, and want an account before showing anything.

## Gains
- A short this-week list on Monday.
- A warning before I put two bad neighbours together.
- Seeing three years of history for a bed in February.
- A layout idea to start from, that I can change.
- Photos that land next to the bed they show.

## Products and services
- Square-foot bed planner with a crop picker.
- Crop catalogue with timing and companion relations.
- Calendar from my frost dates.
- Journal with notes and photos.
- AI layout proposal for a bed.

## Pain relievers
- Frost dates the user sets, with a regional default.
- Companion relations shown with their source.
- Journal entries attached to a bed and a planting.
- AI proposal shown as a preview with apply / discard. (Matches N02.)
- Catalogue browsable without an account.

## Gain creators
- Dashboard of tasks for this week, computed from the calendar.
- Conflict badges on the grid while placing crops.
- Rotation warning from previous seasons. (Not in current scope; see the 2026-10-15 interview.)
- Balcony boxes as small grids in centimetres. (Conflicts with N01 unless a box is a grid.)

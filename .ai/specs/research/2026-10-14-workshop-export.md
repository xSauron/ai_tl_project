# Workshop — 2026-10-14, project owner and one friend who gardens
- Decision the session had to produce: what is the smallest Plant.AI that is fun to build and that one real gardener would open more than once.
- Decider: project owner.
- Constraints: 25 hours of build time for the MVP (course pace); hobby project, so no payments, no ads, no seed shop; one AI feature at most in the MVP; stack fixed by the course (Next.js, PostgreSQL, MikroORM, awilix).
- Decisions taken: D — beds are square-foot grids, no freehand drawing (owner, "a grid I can finish, a drawing tool I can't"); D — the AI only suggests, the user presses apply (both, "I want to blame myself, not the robot").
- Open questions: where does the crop catalogue come from, and who checks it? (blocking, owner); do frost dates come from a region table or does the user type them? (non-blocking); is crop rotation in the MVP? (non-blocking, raised by the friend).

# Interview guide — a gardener's last season

Script for E01-T01 (#18). It tests A01 (do growers other than the owner lose track of tasks, timing, companions or pruning?) and collects what Q07 needs (when they lose track, is it *due*, *done* or *how*?). Write each interview up with `interview-note.md`, then add the **Guide checks** block from the end of this file. Combine the notes with `interview-synthesis.md`.

Spoken questions are in Polish, because the interviews are held in Polish. The English gloss follows each one.

## Before the interview

- Who: a gardener the owner knows with at least one season behind them. Aim for at least one person who grows bushes or trees (D02, A04).
- Length: 20–30 minutes, in person or by phone. Take notes, or record with consent.
- Privacy: the note names the person by **role only** (for example "allotment holder, 3 seasons, vegetables and two plum trees"). No names, addresses or contact details.
- **Don't mention the app, the task list or AI before part 4.** If they ask, say you're "collecting stories about gardening seasons" and that you'll show something at the end.
- Ask about what happened, not opinions or wishes. Avoid "would you use…", "would it help if…" and "is X a problem for you?".
- Write down the **time** when part 4 starts. Everything said before that is unprompted.

## Part 1 — Context (2 min)

1. „Co uprawiasz i gdzie? Ile grządek, krzewów, drzew?” (What do you grow and where? How many beds, bushes, trees?)
2. „Od ilu sezonów?” (For how many seasons?)

## Part 2 — The last time something went wrong (10–15 min, the core)

3. „Opowiedz o ostatnim razie, kiedy coś w ogrodzie zrobiłeś za późno, za wcześnie albo wcale.” (Tell me about the last time you did something in the garden too late, too early or not at all.)
   - If they generalise ("zawsze…", "zwykle…"), bring them back: „A ostatnio, konkretnie kiedy?” (And most recently, when exactly?)
   - If nothing comes up: „A coś, co Cię w zeszłym sezonie zaskoczyło albo poszło nie tak?” (Anything that surprised you or went wrong last season?)
4. Follow-ups on that episode, one at a time:
   - „Co to było i kiedy się zorientowałeś?” (What was it, and when did you notice?)
   - „Co z tego wynikło?” (What came of it? This is the cost: a lost crop, a damaged plant, nothing.)
   - „Skąd wiedziałeś, kiedy to powinno być zrobione?” (How did you know when it should be done?)
   - „Jak to pamiętasz albo zapisujesz?” (How do you remember or note it down? Notebook, phone, memory, packets.)
   - „Robiłeś to już wcześniej?” (Had you done it before?)
5. „A jeszcze jeden taki raz?” (One more time like that?) Repeat 4 for up to two episodes.

**Don't read out the three Q07 options.** After the interview, classify each episode yourself (see Guide checks):
- **due**: they didn't know or forgot that it was time
- **done**: they didn't know whether it had already been done
- **how**: they knew it was time but not how to do it (for example, how to cut)
- **other**: none of these; describe it

## Part 3 — Topics not yet raised (5–8 min)

Ask only about topics they **haven't** brought up. In the note, mark each answer *unprompted* (it came up in part 2) or *prompted* (asked here).

6. Companions: „Ostatnim razem, kiedy decydowałeś, co posadzić obok czego — jak to zrobiłeś?” (Last time you decided what to plant next to what, how did you do it?) H03 follow-up: „Korzystałeś z jakiejś porady? Co z nią zrobiłeś?” (Did you use any advice? What did you do with it?)
7. Pruning: „Kiedy ostatnio coś przycinałeś? Skąd wiedziałeś kiedy i jak? Zdarzyło się, że cięcie zaszkodziło?” (When did you last prune something? How did you know when and how? Has a cut ever done harm?)
8. Spraying (D09 review): „Kiedy ostatnio robiłeś oprysk? Skąd wiedziałeś, jaki i kiedy?” (When did you last spray? How did you know which spray and when?)
9. Notes from earlier seasons (D09 review): „Wracasz do tego, co było w poprzednich latach? Jak?” (Do you go back to what happened in earlier years? How?)
10. Frost dates (H05): „Skąd wiedziałeś, kiedy wysadzić rozsadę na zewnątrz zeszłej wiosny?” (How did you know when to plant seedlings out last spring?)

## Part 4 — Paper flow (5 min, after everything above)

Note the time. Show the steps on paper, one card at a time, using **their own garden** from part 1. At each card, note where they hesitate, question it or would stop (H01).

1. Card: "Enter your frost dates" (the last spring frost, the first autumn frost)
2. Card: "Add the plants you grow" (vegetables, bushes, trees)
3. Card: "Your task list: sow / plant out / prune, with dates"
4. Card: "Lay your plants out on a square-foot bed; neighbour warnings appear"
5. Card: "Ask a question about a plant in your garden; the answer is labelled a suggestion"

Checks:
- H02: at card 4, „Gdzie tu trafi Twoje drzewo/krzew?” (Where would your tree/bush go here?)
- H04: show **task card A** (no source), then **task card B** (the same task with a source). „Co byś zrobił z tym zadaniem?” (What would you do with this task?) Write down what they say they'd do, and why.
  - Before the interviews, fill in both cards from the owner's own D04 catalogue entry for one plant the gardener grows. Don't invent the window.
  - Card A: "Prune {plant}: {window from the catalogue entry}"
  - Card B: the same, plus "Source: {citation from the catalogue entry}"
- H06: at card 3, „Jak byś zapamiętał, że to już zrobione?” (How would you remember that this is already done?)

## Part 5 — Close (1 min)

11. „Czy jest coś, o co nie zapytałem, a powinienem?” (Is there anything I didn't ask that I should have?)
12. „Mogę wrócić z pytaniem za kilka tygodni?” (May I come back with a question in a few weeks?) Write down yes or no only, no contact details.

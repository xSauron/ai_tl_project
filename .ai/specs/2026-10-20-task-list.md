# Task list, frost dates and operations

Status: skeleton — blocked on Open Questions. Serves E02-S02 (#24), E02-S04 (#26), E02-S05 (#27) of epic E02 (#19).

## 📝 TLDR
The owner loses track of sowing, planting-out and pruning dates across many plants. **Proposed (future):** the owner sets frost dates per garden; the app derives sowing, planting-out and pruning windows from catalogue timing and those dates, never storing them (R05); and one operations list records what was or will be done, once for many plants (D14, R09).
